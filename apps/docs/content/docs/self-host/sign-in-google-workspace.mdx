---
title: Sign In with Google Workspace
description: Company sign-in through Google Workspace, step by step.
---

This sets up [company sign-in](/self-host/sign-in#company-sign-in-oidc) with your Google Workspace accounts. You need a Google Cloud project that belongs to your organization, and your BugBerry address (`APP_URL`).

If [Google](/self-host/sign-in#google) is already on, you don't need this page: both sign people in with the same Google accounts. Company sign-in puts your own name on the button, and one of the two is enough.

## In Google Cloud

1. In the Google Cloud console, pick a project inside your organization and open **Google Auth Platform**. If the project has never been set up for sign-in, select **Get started** and fill in the app's name and a support email.
2. Under **Audience**, set **User type** to **Internal**. Only accounts in your organization can then sign in.
3. Under **Clients**, select **Create client** and choose **Web application**.
4. Add the **Authorized redirect URI** `<APP_URL>/api/auth/callback/oidc`, for example `https://bugberry.example.com/api/auth/callback/oidc`. Create the client.
5. Copy the **Client ID** and the **Client secret**.

With **External** in place of **Internal**, anyone with a Google account gets as far as BugBerry, and only `ALLOWED_EMAIL_DOMAINS` decides who may sign in.

## In BugBerry

Add to `.env`, then restart with `docker compose up -d`:

```bash
OIDC_ISSUER=https://accounts.google.com
OIDC_CLIENT_ID=<the client ID>
OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET=<the client secret>
OIDC_NAME="Example"
```

The sign-in page now shows **Continue with Example**.

## If It Doesn't Work

| What you see | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| Google says "Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch" | The redirect URI in the client doesn't match `<APP_URL>/api/auth/callback/oidc` exactly. |
| Google says "Error 403: org_internal" | The account is outside your organization, and the app's user type is **Internal**. |
| "Company sign-in isn't reachable right now" | BugBerry can't reach `accounts.google.com` from inside its container. |
| "Sign-in couldn't be completed" | `OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET` doesn't match the client's secret. |
| "This email can't sign in" | The address is outside `ALLOWED_EMAIL_DOMAINS`. |
