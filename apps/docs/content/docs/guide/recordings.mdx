title: Recordings

A recording shows the bug happening, and the report lines up the console, the network, and your actions with the video.

## Start a recording

Click the BugBerry icon, choose what to record under **Record**, then press the big button:

- **Tab:** this tab, with its sound.
- **Screen** or **Window:** Chrome asks which one. The console, network, and actions come from the tab you started on and from every tab you go to while recording, in one timeline. Tabs you never open during the recording aren't included, nor are pages that aren't web pages (a new tab, `chrome://…`).

<Screenshot src="/screenshots/recordings/popup.png" alt="The BugBerry popup with Tab chosen under Record, the Record this tab button, and the Mic off and 3s countdown chips" width={380} />

The chips under the button set the rest. **Mic off** opens the list of microphones: pick one to add your voice. **System audio** adds sound from other apps when you record the screen. The first time you turn on a microphone, BugBerry asks over the page you're on, then the browser asks for permission once.

Recording starts after a 3-second countdown. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels it; the **3s countdown** chip turns it off. The popup remembers your choices for next time and for the shortcuts.

## While it records

A **Recording Bar** sits on the page. Drag it by its left edge; it stays where you put it, even after the page reloads. It shows the time and how many errors and failed requests were caught so far, and has:

- **Pause:** nothing is recorded meanwhile, not the video and not the console, network, or actions, so you can type private data.
- **Microphone:** on or off.
- **Draw:** pen, arrow, and circle marks that fade after 3 seconds, and numbered **notes** that stay and go into the report with their time in the video. **Clear** takes the marks away; <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves Draw.
- **Restart** and **Discard**, each after a confirmation.
- **Stop.**

<Screenshot src="/screenshots/recordings/draw.png" alt="Draw mode on the checkout page: an arrow pointing at the Pay button, and note 1 saying Pay does nothing, no error shown, with the drawing tools above the bar" />

A "REC" badge shows on the BugBerry icon while recording.

## Stop

Any of these stops the recording:

- **Stop** on the Recording Bar;
- **Stop and review** in the popup: click the BugBerry icon or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) while recording, and the popup shows the recording's controls;
- <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>;
- the browser's own **Stop sharing**.

<Screenshot src="/screenshots/recordings/popup-recording.png" alt="The popup while recording: REC, Recording this tab, the time, Stop and review, then Pause, Restart and Discard, and the counts of errors, failed requests and actions" width={380} />

The popup shows the same controls as the bar, and the counts of errors, failed requests, and actions so far. Closing it keeps recording.

A recording also stops by itself after 20 minutes of recording time (pauses don't count), before it would pass the server's upload limit (or, on BugBerry cloud, the workspace's file size or space left), or when the recorded tab is closed.

Recording carries on when the tab goes to another page. The report then notes that the console, network, and actions from the earlier page aren't included.

## Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) | Open BugBerry; while recording, its recording controls |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> | Screenshot, with your last choice |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> | Record, with your last choice |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> | Stop recording |

On Firefox, opening BugBerry is <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>; see [BugBerry on Firefox](/guide/firefox).
