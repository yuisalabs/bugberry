title: Review and send

After a screenshot or a recording, **Review your report** opens over the page. On pages extensions can't reach, such as a new tab or a PDF, it opens in a tab of its own.

## What you see

- The video or the screenshot, with the annotation tools for screenshots.
- What the logs caught, in cards under it: **Console** errors, **Network** failures, **Actions** (your steps), and the **Browser**.
- A **title**, suggested from the first failed request or error. Change it if it doesn't say what went wrong.

## Fill in the details

- **Save to:** the workspace the report goes to. It starts on the workspace open in the dashboard.
- **What went wrong?** What you expected, and what happened instead.
- **Project:** optional.
- **Priority:** optional.
- **Assignee:** optional; anyone in the workspace.
- **Notes:** the notes made while recording, with their time in the video. Edit or remove them here.
- **Include my city:** optional, and only when the review opens in a tab of its own. See [Privacy and security](/privacy-security#your-city).

## Trim a recording

Under a recording, a strip shows its frames. Drag the strip's left and right edges to keep only the part that shows the bug; the video jumps to the frame at the edge you drag, and the play button plays just that part.

- Only the part you keep is sent. The parts cut off never leave your computer.
- The console, network, actions, notes and pauses follow the trimmed part, and so do the report's length and preview.
- The trimmed video is made when you press **Create report**; a bar shows **Preparing video…** first. If it can't be made, **Send full video** sends the whole recording instead.
- Once a report has started sending, its trim can't be changed: the strip says the video has already started sending.

## Send it, or not yet

- **Create report** sends it. The review stays over your page and shows the report, with its share link already copied. From there you can:
  - change **who can see it**: **Workspace members** (Workspace), **Anyone at @yourcompany.com** with your own email domain (Internal), or **Anyone with the link** (Public); see [Workspaces and access](/guide/workspaces-and-access#who-can-see-a-report);
  - **Open report** in the dashboard;
  - send it to one of the project's trackers;
  - **Record another** or **Take another** on the same page, or **Done**.
- **Record again** or **Retake** starts over. The current recording or screenshot isn't kept.
- Closing the review keeps the report as a draft. Find it under **Unsent drafts** in the popup's **⋯** menu.
