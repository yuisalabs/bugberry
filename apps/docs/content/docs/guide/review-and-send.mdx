---
title: Review and send
description: Check what was caught, describe the bug, and create the report.
---

After a screenshot or a recording, **Review your report** opens over the page. On pages extensions can't reach, such as a new tab or a PDF, it opens in a tab of its own.

## What you see

- The video or the screenshot, with the annotation tools for screenshots.
- What the logs caught: console errors, failed requests, your steps, and the browser.
- A **title**, suggested from the first failed request or error. Change it if it doesn't say what went wrong.

## Fill in the details

- **Save to:** the workspace the report goes to.
- **What went wrong?** What you expected, and what happened instead.
- **Project:** optional.
- **Priority:** optional.
- **Assignee:** optional; anyone in the workspace.
- **Include my city:** optional, and only when the review opens in a tab of its own. See [Privacy and security](/privacy-security#your-city).

## Send it, or not yet

- **Create report** sends it. The review stays over your page and shows the report, with its link already copied. From there you can:
  - change **who can see it**: Workspace, Internal, or Public (see [Workspaces and access](/guide/workspaces-and-access#who-can-see-a-report));
  - **Open report** in the dashboard;
  - send it to one of the project's trackers;
  - **Record another** or **Take another** on the same page, or **Done**.
- **Record again** or **Retake** starts over. The current recording or screenshot isn't kept.
- Closing the review keeps the report as a draft. Find it under **Unsent drafts** in the popup's menu.
