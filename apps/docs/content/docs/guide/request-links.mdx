---
title: Request Links
description: Ask someone outside the team for a recording, without an account or the extension.
---

A request link is for someone who won't install the extension: a customer, a client, a colleague in another team. They open it in their browser, record their screen or take a screenshot, and it arrives in your workspace as a report.

## Make a Link

On the reports page, press **Request a recording**. Choose:

- **Project:** where the report is filed. The project's page picks itself.
- **Note for them:** what you want them to show. They read it when they open the link.
- **Page address:** optional, the page you want them to record. It's shown to them as a link and kept on the report.
- **Works for:** 1, 7 or 30 days.
- **Takes:** 1, 5 or 20 recordings.

Press **Create link**, then **Copy**. The link is shown once: BugBerry keeps only a hash of it, so it can't show the link again. Lost it? Make a new one.

A workspace can have up to 50 links that still take recordings.

## What the Other Person Sees

The page names you, your workspace and your note. Their browser asks what to share (a tab, a window or the whole screen), and only that is recorded. Nothing is sent until they press **Send**, after typing their name. Their email and a description are optional.

- They need a computer: phones can't record the screen from a browser. Chrome, Edge and Firefox record video; a browser that can't record WebM, such as an older Safari, sends a screenshot instead.
- A recording can be up to 5 minutes (less if your workspace's own recording limit is shorter) and 95 MB.
- If they reload the page while it uploads, the same upload goes on.
- If the link stops working before they press Send, the page lets them save the recording as a file.

## What Arrives

The report is filed in your name, as its reporter, and says **Sent by** with the name they typed. Members also see the email they gave.

- A video or a screenshot, and nothing else: no console, network or actions, since the page can't see what happens in their other tabs.
- You get a notification in the bell and an email. The workspace's Slack, Discord, Telegram and webhook channels hear about it like any new report.
- It's **not** sent to the project's trackers by itself, even with auto-send on. Look at it first, then send it from the report's page.
- Only workspace members can see it. The sender never gets a link to the report.

## Links in This Workspace

The same dialog lists the workspace's links: how many recordings each took, when it expires, and the reports that came through it. Whoever made a link, or an admin, can **Revoke** it. A revoked link stops taking recordings at once; reports already sent stay.

A link also stops working when the member who made it leaves the workspace or is deactivated.

On a self-hosted server with S3 storage, the bucket's CORS rule must allow `PUT` from the dashboard's address. See [Storage](/self-host/storage#the-cors-rule).
