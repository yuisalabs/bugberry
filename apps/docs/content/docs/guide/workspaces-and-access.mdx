## Workspaces

Every report, project, and integration belongs to one **workspace**: a team. Someone who signs up without an invitation automatically gets a workspace of their own and becomes its admin.

One person can be in several workspaces. Switch with the picker at the top left of the dashboard, or with **Save to** when you review a report in the extension. The picker also creates a workspace, or joins one with a code.

## Projects and URL patterns

Inside a workspace, **projects** group reports, for example one per app or site. Admins manage them under **Projects** in the dashboard's top bar.

A project can have **URL patterns**: when someone reports a bug on a page whose address matches one, the extension picks that project for them. They can still choose another before sending.

- `*` stands for anything: `*.example.com` covers every subdomain of example.com.
- A pattern without a path, such as `app.example.com`, covers every page on that host.
- When patterns from several projects match, the most specific one wins.

Set them with **Edit** next to a project's patterns, or from its **⋯** menu with **URL patterns**. **Test a URL**, under the projects, shows which project an address would go to.

## Inviting people

Joining someone else's workspace always takes an invitation. A workspace admin opens **Members** in the top bar and types email addresses under **Invite teammates**, choosing **Member** or **Admin**; BugBerry sends each an invitation link. A link is valid for 7 days and only works for the address it was sent to.

Admins can also change a member's role or remove them on the same page. **Who can do what** sums up the roles: an **Owner** can delete the workspace, an **Admin** manages members, projects and integrations, and a **Member** records, views and comments on reports.

## Who can see a report

Every report has one of three access levels:

| Level | Who can open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** (default) | Members of the workspace only |
| **Internal** | Anyone signed in with the same email domain as the reporter (for example everyone at `@example.com`), member or not, through the share link. Not available for free email providers such as Gmail. |
| **Public** | Anyone with the share link, without signing in |

Only the reporter or a workspace admin can change a report's access level: on the report's page, open the arrow next to **Copy link**, under **Who can open this link**.

## Your city

When reviewing a report, a reporter can tick **Include my city**. The city is shown to workspace members only, never on share links or in tracker issues. See [Privacy and security](/privacy-security#your-city).
