---
title: Screenshots
description: Capture what's on the page, mark it up, and send it with the console and network attached.
---

Click the BugBerry icon in the toolbar (or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) and choose **Capture screenshot**.

## What to capture

The arrow next to **Capture screenshot** holds the choices:

- **Visible area:** everything you can see in the tab.
- **Select area:** drag a box around the part that matters. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

The popup remembers your choice for next time and for the shortcut <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd>.

## Mark it up

The screenshot opens in **Review your report** with the annotation tools:

| Tool | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Pen | Draw freely |
| Box | Frame an area |
| Arrow | Point at something |
| Text | Write a short note on the image |
| Blur | Hide private data, such as names, emails, or numbers |

Pick a color for the pen, box, arrow, and text. The image is sent with your marks and blurs already drawn into it.

## What comes with it

Even with a screenshot, the report carries what the page did before you captured it: console messages and errors, network requests (failed ones are flagged), your clicks and keys, and details about the browser, the screen, and the page.
