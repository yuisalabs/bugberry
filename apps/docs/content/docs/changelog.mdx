Each entry is named after the browser extension's version. Changes to the dashboard and the server ship with it.

## 0.7.0 · 24 October 2026

- **Your camera in the recording.** When you record a tab, the new **Camera** chip in the popup puts your face in a bubble on the page, so it's in the video. Pick a camera and a shape, **Circle** or **Rectangle**. The bubble shows up as the countdown starts; drag it where you like, and drag its corner to make it bigger or smaller (96 to 320 px high). Its place and size are kept for next time. The camera turns off as soon as the recording ends. See [Recordings](/guide/recordings#your-camera).
- **Trim a recording before sending it.** The review shows a strip of the video's frames: drag its edges to keep only the part that shows the bug. Only that part is sent, and the console, network, actions, notes and pauses follow it. See [Review and send](/guide/review-and-send#trim-a-recording).
- **Send to tracker** has its own green button on a report's page, next to **Copy link**, with the project's trackers inside. On phones it stays in the **More** menu.
- **Choosing who can open a link** no longer flickers while it saves.
- The microphone and camera lists fit in the popup, however many devices you have.
- **Integrations** show as a grid of cards, the card to connect or add one more always last.
- The extension calls a server **BugBerry cloud** only when it is BugBerry's own.
- **Firefox 130 or newer** is needed, the first Firefox that can cut a video for Trim.
- **Self-hosting:** no server change is needed for this version.

## 0.6.0 · 23 October 2026

- **Long recordings get through.** A recording over 16 MB now goes up in 16 MB parts, each its own request, so a server behind Cloudflare takes recordings up to its upload limit (300 MB by default, about 20 minutes) instead of stopping near 100 MB. A part that fails is sent again on its own, not the whole video, and on a slow connection whose upload links run out the extension gets new ones and carries on.
- **You see the upload going.** While a report is sent, a bar above the review's buttons shows how much has gone up, such as "15 MB of 35 MB · 42%", then "Creating report…" while the server finishes it.
- A report sent again in the same moment, after a timeout, no longer sends its notifications and tracker issues twice.
- **Self-hosting:** update the server with the extension. With S3 storage (RustFS, MinIO, R2, AWS), uploads in parts need no setup, and the server drops the parts of an upload left unfinished after a day. Behind Cloudflare, `MAX_UPLOAD_MB` can go back to `300`; keep it at `95` while people still use an extension older than 0.6.0, which sends each file in one request. Local storage also still takes each file in one request. A database migration adds one column when the server starts.

## 0.5.0 · 23 October 2026

- **Storage limits on BugBerry cloud.** Next to 5 screenshots and 5 video recordings, each workspace now keeps up to **250 MB**, and one file may be up to **50 MB**. Every file of a report counts: the screenshot or video, the logs and the preview. Deleting a report frees its space.
- **You see what's left.** The dashboard's usage line, the popup and the review show the storage used. A recording stops by itself before it passes 50 MB or the space left, and the review says when a capture doesn't fit and what to do.
- **The pricing page** lists the new limits.
- **Self-hosting:** no change unless `ACCOUNT_LIMITS=true`. Then `LIMIT_STORAGE_MB` and `LIMIT_FILE_MB` set the sizes; see [Account limits](/self-host/account-limits). Workspaces already over 250 MB keep everything but take no new reports until one is deleted.

## 0.4.1 · 23 October 2026

- **Reports go to the workspace you're working in.** A new screenshot or recording now goes to the workspace open in the dashboard, then to the one you sent to last. Before, the extension could keep sending to your own workspace after you'd moved to a teammate's, and say it was full when it wasn't the one you meant.

## 0.4.0 · 23 October 2026

- **A redesigned review.** The review is wider: your capture sits on the left with a floating toolbar for marking up a screenshot, and what the logs caught shows below it. On the right, **Save to** picks the workspace, then a title, what went wrong, **Project**, **Priority** and the new **Assignee**.
- **Report created, without leaving the page.** After **Create report** the review stays open over your page and shows the report's number, title, project, priority and assignee. Its link is copied at once, you can change who can see it, send it to the project's trackers, or start **Record another** (**Take another** for a screenshot).
- **Retake and Record again say what happens:** the current screenshot or recording isn't kept, and a new one is taken of the same page.
- **No more new tabs.** Allowing the microphone, the extension's settings, an unsent draft and switching servers from a dashboard now open over the page you're on. Only pages extensions can't reach (a new tab, `chrome://…`, the Web Store, PDFs) and Firefox still use a tab of their own.
- **Window and screen recordings follow you across tabs.** The console, network and actions now come from every tab you go to while recording, not only the tab you started on.
- **Select area shows its size** next to the cursor while you drag.
- The popup no longer flickers when it opens.

## 0.3.0 · 23 October 2026

- **A new dashboard layout.** The sidebar is gone: a top bar holds your workspace, All reports, AI Agents, Projects, Members and Integrations, with the bell, Invite, New report and your account menu on the right. Every page sits on one panel and opens with the BugBerry logo. On phones the same pages open from a menu.
- **All reports** has a wider search, filters and sorting that stand out, and up to four cards in a row on wide screens.
- **Projects.** Each project shows its reports, open reports and when the last one came in, and can have its own logo. **Test a URL** tells you which project the extension files a page under, and **Pages without a project** lists the sites whose reports no pattern catches, with the project to add them to. The top bar's Projects menu can be searched.
- **Members.** Search members and pending invites in one place, change roles from a menu, and **invite people as Member or Admin**. A short guide says what each role can do.
- **Integrations.** Connections are grouped into trackers and notifications, every provider is listed with what it does, and a provider you already use can be connected again (a second GitHub account, another Slack channel). A project's Integrations page shows the connections ready to use there.
- **Workspace and Account settings** are split into tabs.
- **A new report page.** The recording and what it's about sit on the left; a sidebar on the right has **Details** (status, priority, assignee, reporter, project, created, then the comments) and **DevTools**, which the page opens on. DevTools starts on **Overview**, and **Activity** is the new name for Actions. Drag the sidebar's edge to make it wider or narrower, or hide it from the header.
- **Back to where you were.** Open a report from a project and its back button names that project, with **All reports** beside it; open it from All reports and it goes back there.
- **Add to Chrome.** An empty report list offers the extension when your browser doesn't have it, and **Install** in the account menu always leads to the Chrome Web Store.
- **Error pages.** Clear pages for not found, no access, server errors and maintenance, each with a way back.
- **Self-hosting:** two database changes (project logos and invitation roles) apply on their own when the server starts. Without `EXTENSION_INSTALL_URL`, install links now go to BugBerry's Chrome Web Store page instead of saying "Ask your admin".

## 0.2.6 · 23 October 2026

- **A new public site on BugBerry cloud.** Home, Pricing, Integrations, AI Agents and Request a demo pages explain what BugBerry does and what it costs, with a sticky header, a Product menu and a menu built for phones.
- **Request a demo.** Book 30 minutes with the team from `/demo`; requests are kept and the team gets an email it can reply to directly.
- **Pricing.** BugBerry is free during early access, with 5 screenshots and 5 video recordings per workspace; self-hosting has no limits.
- **New headings.** The dashboard and the extension use Sora for headings; text stays in DM Sans and code in Geist Mono.
- **Privacy, security and terms** are on one page at `/privacy`, and it now says exactly what is kept out of a report before upload.
- **Self-hosting:** the marketing pages are off unless `PUBLIC_SITE=true`, so `/` on your own server goes straight to sign-in. `DEMO_REQUEST_EMAIL` names who gets demo requests. See [Environment variables](/self-host/environment).
- This changelog.

## 0.2.5 · 22 October 2026

- **A new popup.** Pick a tab, a window or the whole screen from tiles, choose a microphone with a live level meter, and pause, resume or stop a recording right from the toolbar.
- **Workspace connections.** Connect GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear, Plane, ActiveCollab, Slack, Discord, Telegram or a webhook once for the workspace, then pick per project where its reports go.
- **New integrations.** GitLab, Jira Cloud, Linear and Plane (cloud and self-hosted) as trackers; Slack, Discord, Telegram and signed webhooks for new-report notifications.
- **Comment threads.** Reply to a comment, react with emoji, and follow a report's activity. Replies show up in the bell.
- **A report says whether it reached its tracker**, and its fields change at once instead of flickering.
- **Phones.** The report page, its menu and the settings pages were redesigned for small screens.
- **Docs** now live at `/docs`, with search.

## 0.2.4 · 20 October 2026

- A public privacy policy at `/privacy`, linked from the landing page.
- The review never shows an out-of-date "workspace full" state, and **Open dashboard** can't be blocked as a popup.

## 0.2.3 · 20 October 2026

- **Account limits on BugBerry cloud.** Each account owns one workspace with 5 screenshots and 5 video recordings. The popup shows how much is used, and the review warns before a report would go over.
- Self-hosted servers have no limits; an administrator can mark accounts as unlimited.

## 0.2.2 · 20 October 2026

- In Firefox, the review over a page lists your workspaces again.

## 0.2.1 · 20 October 2026

- Recordings with sound name their audio codec, so more players can open them.

## 0.2.0 · 20 October 2026

- **Firefox and Opera.** A signed Firefox build records through a recorder window; Opera and other browsers without tab capture record a window or the screen instead.
- **AI agents (MCP).** Connect Claude, Cursor and other AI apps from **Settings → AI Agents**, approve them on a consent page, and copy a report for an AI in one click.
- Recordings play in Safari.
- A click on a row in **Actions** or **Console** plays the video from that moment, and a network request opens under the table instead of replacing it.
