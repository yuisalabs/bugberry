title: 0.8.0
description: Released 24 October 2026.

- **Try it before signing in.** Right after installing the extension you can take screenshots and record. The review opens as usual; in place of **Create report** it says **Sign in to send**, and the capture waits in the popup's drafts. Sign in, and it is sent from there. See [Review and Send](/guide/review-and-send#before-you-sign-in).
- **BugBerry cloud limits recordings by length, not size.** A workspace still keeps 5 screenshots and 5 recordings, and a recording can be up to 2 minutes long. The recording bar shows the limit next to the time (**01:45 / 02:00**), and turns it orange in the last 15 seconds. It stops by itself at 2 minutes, and the review says so; a longer one can be trimmed to fit. Storage and file sizes are no longer limited per account.
- **Self-hosting:** `LIMIT_RECORDING_SECONDS` (default `120`) replaces `LIMIT_STORAGE_MB` and `LIMIT_FILE_MB`, which are no longer read. It only matters with `ACCOUNT_LIMITS=true`. See [Account Limits](/self-host/account-limits).
