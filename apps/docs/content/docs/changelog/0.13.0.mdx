---
title: 0.13.0
description: Released 15 November 2026.
---

- **Sign in with your company's own login.** A self-hosted server can now use one OpenID Connect identity provider:
  Keycloak, Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Authentik, Google Workspace and others. The sign-in page shows a
  **Continue with …** button under the name you choose. Someone who already has an account reaches it when they sign
  in through the provider with the same email address. The extension and AI agents sign in the same way, with nothing
  to change on their side. See [Company sign-in](/self-host/sign-in#company-sign-in-oidc) and the
  [Keycloak guide](/self-host/sign-in-keycloak).
- **SSO only, without losing email.** `EMAIL_SIGN_IN=false` turns off signing in with an emailed code while
  invitations and notifications keep sending. Before, the only way to turn codes off was to turn all email off.
- **Cut someone off when they leave.** `deactivate-user` signs an account out of every browser and extension, revokes
  its tokens, disconnects its AI apps, and refuses it on every sign-in method from the next request on. Its reports
  and comments stay. On the **Members** page the person is marked **Deactivated** and no longer appears when
  assigning or mentioning. `reactivate-user` lets them back in, and `promote-admin` gives a workspace a new admin
  when its only one left. See [When Someone Leaves](/self-host/operations#when-someone-leaves).
- **Self-hosting:** update the server and the dashboard with `docker compose up -d --build`. Migration `0036` runs
  when the server starts. Nothing changes until you set the new variables: `OIDC_ISSUER`, `OIDC_CLIENT_ID`,
  `OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET`, `OIDC_NAME` and `EMAIL_SIGN_IN`. The extension stays at 0.12.2.
