title: 0.9.0
description: Released 26 October 2026.
---

- **A Getting started checklist for new accounts.** Right after signing up, a checklist floating in the bottom left corner of **All reports** shows five steps: install the extension, take a screenshot, record a video, look at DevTools in a report, and share it. Each step ticks itself when you do it, in any order, and the one to do next has its own button. On other pages it waits as a small pill in the same corner, and on a report's page the **Console** and **Network** tabs, then **Copy link**, light up when it's their turn. After the five, a short celebration, then bonus missions: invite a teammate, create a project, comment, connect a tracker, send a report to it, connect an AI agent. Collapse it whenever you like; it goes away once every mission is done. Accounts made before this version don't see it. See [Dashboard](/guide/dashboard#getting-started).
- **Visited URLs say what went wrong on each page.** In a report's **Overview**, each page now shows its errors and failed requests (for example **2 errors** and **1 failed request**) in place of the **Bug here** mark. Pressing one opens **Console** or **Network** with only what happened on that page, until **Show all**. Closed, the list shows the first page where something went wrong.
- **Write the report with AI, using your own OpenAI-compatible key.** In the review of a recording, **Write with AI** drafts the title and the steps to reproduce from what the recording caught, laid out by your workspace's template; **Undo** brings back what you had typed. A workspace admin sets a team key in **AI Agents → Write with AI**, or you add your own in the extension's settings, which can even be a model on `localhost`. Response bodies are only sent if an admin turns that on. See [Write with AI](/guide/ai-writing).
- **Send to a tracker right after sending, even without a project.** When a report goes out with no project, the review now offers **Project** under **Open report**: pick one, and that project's trackers show up, ready to send. Each tracker shows its integration's logo (GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear, Plane, ActiveCollab). See [Review and Send](/guide/review-and-send#send-it-or-not-yet).
- **Notes are ready to type and fade like the drawings.** In Draw, clicking with **Note** puts the cursor straight in the note, and a saved note fades from the page after 5 seconds; the report keeps it with its time in the video.
- **A steadier review.** A long title wraps instead of running off the edge; the description scrolls inside its own box, so **Save to** stays at the top and **Project**, **Priority** and **Assignee** stay in view below it.
- **The recording bar no longer blinks** when **Draw** or the **Restart** and **Discard** confirmations open above it.
- **Self-hosting:** database migrations run when the server starts: the checklist's column, and the tables and columns for AI keys and templates. The team key is encrypted with the existing `ENCRYPTION_KEY`. Write with AI needs the new extension version.
